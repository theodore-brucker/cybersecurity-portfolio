Since my last post, I earned my CISSP, got a security clearance, and switched jobs twice. Through my experience I have come to better understand what is required to be an effective cybersecurity professional who focuses on what matters.

Being able to understand an organization's cybersecurity posture and the threat model it faces is absolutely foundational to an effective cybersecurity program. I have seen an organization focus months of hard work and hundreds of thousands of dollars on security theater before starting the basics like collecting logs, deploying a backup solution, or understanding their regulatory requirements. This resulted in data spills, failed audits, and loss of talented people. Organizations that take the mantle on understanding and mitigating risk, whether it be a high profile CISO, a busy IT manager, or even a help desk technician, will continue to spend less on operations that are more resilient to the abundant threats of tomorrow.

## What's next
After my experiences, I decided to seek mentorship and professional development from top professionals in the cybersecurity field, this time with a GRC spin. I hope that this teaches me how to communicate risk effectively, identify solutions to those risks, and contribute to a secure and safe future for the internet and those who inhabit it.
